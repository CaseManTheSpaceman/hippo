# Skin by hippO — Coming Soon (v1.0.0)

The first release of the Skin by hippO landing page, built to the hippocraticOath brand guidelines.

The page opens on a field of hippos that clears away to leave the logo, then introduces the headline and illustration in sequence. The headline settles on "Coming soon." and the email signup offers itself shortly after — or on hover, tap, or focus, whichever comes first. The sequence loops while the page sits idle and stops as soon as a visitor engages.

**What's included**

- Animated coming-soon page in Cream, Dark Blue, and Hippo Purple, with self-hosted Petrona and Quicksand
- Email signup posting to a Google Sheet, with a confirmation state and bot protection
- A mobile treatment that crops the hippo to its front half and bleeds it off the edge, with stacked full-width form controls
- Open Graph and Twitter Card tags with a share image, plus a complete favicon set
- All layout, spacing, and animation timings exposed as documented CSS variables in one block at the top of the file

**Deploying**

A static site — no build step. Publish the folder root to Vercel and set your form endpoint in `FORM_ENDPOINT` near the top of the script in `index.html`.
